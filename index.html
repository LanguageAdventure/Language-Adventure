About the game

Language Adventure is an independent point-and-click adventure built with Visionaire Studio. Instead of drills and flashcards, you learn English in context: through story, dialogue and exploration.

Features

Dual subtitles

Follow the dialogue in English alongside your own language.

Voiced dialogue

Hear natural spoken English from the characters you meet.

Learn by doing

Puzzles and conversations make vocabulary stick.
