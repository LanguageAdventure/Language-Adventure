I'm Chris a solo developer from the UK and I've been making Language Adventure. It's a hand-made point-and-click adventure game that also helps you learn English. It has subtitles in Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian.

An alien called Lingo has crash-landed in your garden and can't speak English yet. Explore a handmade town that's all filmed in stop motion, solve puzzles and help Lingo get home. Click on anything to hear what it is, and something funny about it, narrated by Adam Buxton.

A free demo is coming to Steam and itch.io for Windows, Mac & Linux on 13th January, but you can play it early by joining the play test through the form on the website.
