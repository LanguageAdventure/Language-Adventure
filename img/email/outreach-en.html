I'm Chris and I've been making Language Adventure. It's a language learning game to help learn English.

It's all handmade and filmed in stop motion! You can explore a small town where everything is labelled and has descriptions with helpful vocabulary.

A demo is coming to Steam and itch.io on 13 January, but you can play it early by joining the playtest through the form on the website: languageadventure.info
You don't have to actually playtest the game, so feel free to join even if you're just curious.
