I'm Chris, a solo developer at Wonky Games, and I've been making Language Adventure. It's a point-and-click adventure game to help learn English, for beginner and intermediate learners of all ages.

You can explore a handmade town that's all filmed in stop motion. Everything is labelled and has descriptions with helpful vocabulary. Click on anything to hear what it is in spoken English, narrated by Adam Buxton.

Here's the trailer: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wJoiifm54UY
